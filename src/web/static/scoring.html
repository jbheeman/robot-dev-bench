Scoring Explained

How biomechanical metrics are calculated and weighed

← Back to Dashboard

Score Calculation

The overall score is a weighted average of individual metric scores, normalized between 0.0 (Experimental) and 1.0 (Superhuman/Industrial).

For each metric, we define an Ideal bound and an Acceptable bound. The actual measured value is linearly interpolated between these two bounds. If a metric exceeds the ideal bound, it is capped at 1.0. If it falls below the acceptable bound, it receives a 0.0.

Task-Specific Weights

Metrics are weighed differently depending on the selected task, dynamically tailoring the evaluation.

Walking

  • Avg Foot Clearance 25%
  • Stride Length 25%
  • Walking Speed 25%
  • Torso Oscillation 25%

Reaching

  • Smoothness (LDLJ) 30%
  • Smoothness (SPARC) 30%
  • Range of Motion 30%
  • Symmetry 10%

Manipulation

  • Range of Motion 50%
  • Smoothness (LDLJ) 25%
  • Smoothness (SPARC) 25%

Jumping

  • Flight Time 40%
  • Peak Z-Accel 30%
  • Landing Jerk 30%

Transitions

  • CoM Oscillation 50%
  • Transition Time 30%
  • Smoothness (LDLJ) 10%
  • Smoothness (SPARC) 10%
