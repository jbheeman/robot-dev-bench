Scoring Explained

How biomechanical metrics are calculated and weighed

Score Calculation

The overall score is a weighted average of individual metric scores, normalized relative to 0.0 (Infant) and 1.0 (Adult) bounds.

For each metric, we define an Ideal bound and an Acceptable bound. The actual measured value is linearly interpolated between these two bounds. If a metric vastly outperforms the ideal bound, its score is uncapped and continues to accumulate points—allowing exceptional performances to score well beyond 1.0!

You can see exactly how many points each metric contributed to your final score by looking at the small green badges (e.g., +0.25 pts) next to the metric values on the dashboard.

Task-Specific Weights

Metrics are weighed differently depending on the selected task, dynamically tailoring the evaluation.

Walking

  • Avg Foot Clearance 25%
  • Stride Length 25%
  • Walking Speed 25%
  • Torso Oscillation 25%

Jumping

  • Flight Time 40%
  • Peak Z-Accel 30%
  • Landing Jerk 30%

Transitions

  • CoM Oscillation 50%
  • Transition Time 30%
  • Smoothness (LDLJ) 10%
  • Smoothness (SPARC) 10%

Manipulation

  • Task Duration 30%
  • Path Efficiency 30%
  • Min Distance to Target 20%
  • Wrist Jerk 20%
